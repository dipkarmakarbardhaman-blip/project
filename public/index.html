Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Quick answers to help you get the best image results.

What image formats are supported?
You can compress JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images up to 15 MB. The compressor exports clean, lightweight JPEG binaries designed for lightning-fast web loading.
What quality preset should I use?
For standard websites and portfolios, 75% (Balanced) yields the highest file size savings (typically 60%–80%) with virtually no perceptible visual loss. For photography and art, use 85% (High).
How does the AWS S3 upload work?
When configured in your .env file, clicking "Upload to AWS S3" sends the optimized image to your private bucket and returns an authenticated signed URL valid for 1 hour.
